# E-invoices (backend)

- **E-invoices** (`src/einvoice`, `EinvoiceModule`; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-hoa-don-dien-tu-design.md`): a paid bill is split into **small invoices** (`Einvoice`; each belongs to one bill, or to none for a **free invoice** (`orderId` null: `POST /einvoices?branch=` without `orderId` or with `orderId: null`, on the business day it is made; the relation keeps `onDelete: Restrict`; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-hddt-bo-cuc-va-hd-tu-do-design.md`); its amount is typed by the user, includes VAT, and may be 0 on a draft while issuing needs ≥ 1) that are issued through the provider **Minvoice**; we only create invoices and keep their number (no cancel/adjust/replace, no PDF, no tax-authority code). Rights (`src/auth/roles.ts`): `EINVOICE_READERS` = `SALES_READERS` (managers, cashier, BOARD), `EINVOICE_WRITERS` = `SALES` (create, edit, delete drafts, look up a buyer's MST; `GET /einvoices/bills`, the page's bill list, is on `EINVOICE_READERS` so HĐQT sees it too), and `CHAIN_ONLY` for issuing, resolving, editing a number, the Minvoice login and the symbol; the branch goes through `BranchScopeService`, and `?branch=` is a query param on every route, `POST /einvoice/config/login` and `PUT /einvoice/config/symbol` included. Routes: `/einvoice/{config, config/login, config/symbols, config/symbol, tax-payers/:taxCode}` (`EinvoiceConfigController`) and `/einvoices` (list, `summary`, `bills`, `bill/:orderId`, `:id`, POST, PATCH, DELETE, `:id/issue`, `:id/resolve`, `:id/number`; the fixed paths are declared before `:id`). `Branch.taxCode` is the MST (`^\d{10}(-\d{3})?$`, not unique: branches may share a company) and becomes part of the Minvoice host name. `EinvoiceConfig` (one row per branch, apart from `Branch` because it holds secrets) keeps the Minvoice username, password and session `{cookie, token, userName}` **encrypted with AES-256-GCM** under `EINVOICE_SECRET` (`einvoice-secret.ts`, `v1:<iv>:<tag>:<ciphertext>`; a key that no longer decrypts reads as `needsLogin`; `EinvoiceModule.onModuleInit` makes production refuse to start without a valid key), plus `symbolCode`, `registerInvoiceId`, the VND `currencyId`, the `seller` profile and `loginError`; it is never returned or logged (`viewOf` picks fields). `needsLogin` is true when there is no config, the stored password cannot be decrypted, there is a `loginError`, or `taxCode ≠ Branch.taxCode`; a missing session alone does not count, since the server logs in again by itself. `Einvoice.status` is `DRAFT` | `SENDING` | `UNCERTAIN` | `ISSUED` (the list's "Lỗi" is a `DRAFT` with `lastError`, filter `status=ERROR`); `draft` (JSON `{buyerAddress, buyerEmail, lines[]}`, at most 50 lines, always read back through `parseDraft`) is **set to null in the same write that makes it `ISSUED`**, which keeps only the header (bill, symbol, date, number, `minvoiceId`, amount, VAT, buyer MST and name, who and when); a draft keeps its own **invoice date** in `invoiceDate` (the calendar day in server time, never the business day: by default the day its bill was paid, `toDateString(endTime)`, or today for a free invoice; `PATCH` changes it only when one is sent), and a send that ends as a `DRAFT`, like `resolve {found: false}`, keeps it; `(sellerTaxCode, symbolCode, invoiceNumber)` is unique; `lastError` holds at most 300 characters.
  The money rules are in `einvoice-math.ts` (pure; `lib/einvoice.ts` in the frontend is a copy, keep them in sync): `Math.round` to the đồng (bills round VAT up instead), `vatRate` ∈ {0, 5, 8, 10}, an invoice can be issued only when its lines add up to `amount` to the đồng (`issueProblem`), and `fillerLine` makes the "Dịch vụ karaoke" line for what is missing, its `vatAmount` off by at most 1 đồng when no price reaches the total (the real Minvoice accepts that: 10.004 đ as filler 9.095 + VAT 909 was issued, 01/10/2026); the sum of a bill's invoices is never blocked, only warned about. `minvoice/minvoice-client.ts` calls the **unofficial web-app API** of Minvoice (cookie + anti-forgery token, mapped by the research project `minvoice-hddt-sender`; it can change without notice and every request lives in that one file): `https://<MST>.minvoice.net` (the MST is checked before it is put in a host name; `MINVOICE_URL_TEMPLATE` replaces the base URL outside production only), `redirect: 'manual'`, timeouts of 10 s for the whole login chain, 10 s per read and 25 s for `POST invoice`. `minvoice-payload.ts` builds the body: `paymentMethod` is always `"TM/CK"` and our reference `K502-<id>` goes in `orderNumber` (`MARKER_FIELD`; checked on the real Minvoice, tenant MST 0107811836, symbol 1C26MTT, on 01/10/2026: it stores `orderNumber`, its invoice list filters by it and its rows carry it — a re-check of an issued invoice found exactly that invoice with `markerSeen`, a reference never sent gave no rows; exact vs. substring match was not told apart, harmless since a row whose `orderNumber` differs is `ambiguous`, never `found`; a 12-digit CCCD as `buyerTaxCode` was not tried on the real one). Minvoice or the lookup failing answers **424**, not 502/504, because Cloudflare replaces an origin's 502/504 page with its own and the Vietnamese message would be lost. The Minvoice login is throttled per branch (`LoginThrottle`, key `einvoice:<branchId>`: 5 wrong passwords in 15 min → 429); ABP's login `result` 2 (or an unknown one) is a wrong password, while 3 (not allowed), 4 (locked out) and 5 (two-factor) are `MinvoiceLoginError` reasons `not-allowed`/`locked`/`two-factor` with their own messages: they never count towards that throttle and a background re-login refused for them stores its own message as `loginError`, not "password changed".
  **Issuing** (`EinvoicesService.issue`): checks first (400/409, nothing changes): the draft parses and adds up, the bill (if any) is not voided, the config is complete, and the **invoice date** is not before the newest `ISSUED` invoice of the same `sellerTaxCode` + `symbolCode` (only a lower bound; a date after today needs `confirmFutureDate: true`, and the year must be the symbol's, characters 3–4); then it locks `DRAFT → SENDING` with `updateMany` on status and `updatedAt` (409 otherwise), writing `sellerTaxCode/symbolCode/registerInvoiceId/invoiceDate` at once, sends **without holding a transaction or a connection**, and writes the outcome. From the lock on it answers **200 with the row**: the outcome is `status` (`ISSUED`, `DRAFT` with `lastError` — the seller, symbol and range cleared, the date kept —, or `UNCERTAIN`). The resend rules are in `einvoice-sender.ts` and `minvoice/classify-send-error.ts`: when Minvoice surely created nothing (`retry`: a 3xx/4xx, a 5xx carrying an ABP `error` body — a body over 2000 characters, such as one with Minvoice's stack trace in `error.details`, is kept as trimmed JSON by `keptBody` so it still parses, and messages drop that stack trace —, a connection that never went out) the sender logs in again (one shared promise per branch), fetches the symbol's current range (`refreshRange`, written back only while the branch still uses that symbol) and resends **once** (`relogin(branchId, taxCode)` refuses a session of another MST than the one locked, so a branch moved to another tenant meanwhile ends as a `DRAFT` with nothing posted), then the row is a `DRAFT` with Minvoice's message; what may have been created is **never resent** (`uncertain`: a network error after the request left, an unreadable 2xx that is not HTML, a 5xx without an ABP `error` body such as a gateway page, a 2xx without id or number), so the row becomes `UNCERTAIN`, which never resends by itself; a **date-order refusal** (`date-order`: Minvoice error code `296` "Ngày hóa đơn phải đảm bảo quy luật tăng dần của số hóa đơn", or the narrow `DATE_ORDER_PATTERNS`, which include the real Minvoice's English HTTP 500 "Create invoice fail because date is [...] use with other invoice before"; Minvoice's ErrorCode-295 (a future date) and 29504 are not date-order) is never retried and ends as a `DRAFT`; a stored password Minvoice refuses on the re-login ends as a `DRAFT` and sets `EinvoiceConfig.loginError`; a number Minvoice gives that one of our rows already holds (P2002) still writes `ISSUED` with a null `invoiceNumber` and a `lastError` asking for the number to be fixed.
  **Stuck and uncertain rows** (they assume **one backend**: never run a second backend against the production database, not even for a test or a migration check, as its start-up sweep would turn the other's live sends into `UNCERTAIN` and its `sending` Set cannot see them): `EinvoicesService.onApplicationBootstrap` turns every `SENDING` row into `UNCERTAIN` at start (one backend, so nothing is running then); a `SENDING` row older than `STALE_SENDING_MS` (3 min) becomes `UNCERTAIN` when it is read through `GET /einvoices/:id` or `GET /einvoices/bill/:orderId` (until then the row stays `SENDING`, and the "Không rõ" tab and `uncertainCount` include `SENDING` rows so a cut-off send is never missing from the pending work; the UI opens a row before offering a check), except ids being sent in this process (the in-process `sending` Set); `record()` logs a failed outcome write (id, kind, number and Minvoice id; never the payload or a secret) and rethrows. Issuing an `UNCERTAIN` row again (**Kiểm tra lại**) first searches Minvoice (`GET /api/api/app/invoice?invoiceSerial&orderNumber&loadAll=true…`, `MinvoiceClient.findByMarker`) for `K502-<id>`: only exactly one candidate of the symbol and date sent counts; a candidate whose row shows `K502-<id>` in `orderNumber` (`markerSeen`, what the real list returns) is recognised and recorded as `ISSUED` on its own, so a lost answer whose invoice was created is found automatically, while one that does not show it is only a hint in `lastError`; `MARKER_SEARCH_CONFIRMED` stays `false` on purpose although the search itself was confirmed on the real Minvoice (01/10/2026): it only gates the **automatic resend after "none found"**, which stays a human decision (**Chưa có — gửi lại**, allowed `RESEND_WAIT_MS` (1 min) after the send), so **nothing is ever resent by itself**; flipping it to `true` would let "none found" resend when the lost send is at least `RESEND_WAIT_MS` old, but needs new UI copy (the "Kiểm tra lại" dialog and `lastError` say the search is not verified) and puts search + send near the 95 s request budget; a found number that clashes with one of ours stays `UNCERTAIN`. `POST /einvoices/:id/resolve {found: true, invoiceNumber} | {found: false}` settles an `UNCERTAIN` row by hand (the only way for a voided bill's); `found: false` (back to `DRAFT`, from where it may be posted again) answers 409 "Lần gửi lúc HH:mm còn quá mới, đợi đến HH:mm…" until `RESEND_WAIT_MS` (1 min; apart from `STALE_SENDING_MS`, which must outlast a send of ≈80 s) after the lost send's `sendingAt` (its `updatedAt` if missing; checked again in the write), since Minvoice may still be saving it. `PATCH /einvoices/:id/number` edits the number of an `ISSUED` one (409 on a taken number, `numberEditedAt/By`, and one log line with the old and new number); numbers have at most 8 digits (`@Max(99_999_999)`).
  **Buyer lookup** (`tax-payer.service.ts`, `GET /einvoice/tax-payers/:taxCode`): the GDT tax portal first (8 s), then `api.xinvoice.vn` (30 s), for an MST of `^(\d{10}(-\d{3})?|\d{12})$`; an in-memory cache of 1000 MST for 7 days, lookups of one MST in flight shared and capped at 50 (429 "Tra cứu nhiều quá, thử lại sau ít giây"), xinvoice at most 10 calls per 30 s (xinvoice's own limit), `redirect: 'manual'`; xinvoice only ever sees the buyer's MST. Lists: `GET /einvoices` (newest 500, `X-Total-Count`; `DRAFT`/`ERROR`/`UNCERTAIN` ignore `from`/`to`, since they are work still to do; `free=1` keeps the free invoices only), `GET /einvoices/bills` (the left column: the paid bills of a range of business days `from`/`to`, default today, or of a bill-number prefix; with `status` the bills holding an invoice of that status, every day for `DRAFT`/`ERROR`/`UNCERTAIN` and voided bills included, and for `ISSUED` the range filters `Einvoice.businessDate` (the bill's business day, or the business day a free invoice was made), not the issue date nor `invoiceDate`; each with what is already split, `allocated`, and `cancelledAt`; 500, `X-Total-Count`), `GET /einvoices/summary` (counts and issued sums in SQL on `ReportPrismaService` with `SharedRequestInterceptor`). `GET /orders/:id` adds `_count.einvoices` and `_count.issuedEinvoices` **for closed bills only** (two small `Einvoice(orderId)` counts; open sessions, which the room page polls, are unchanged) for the warning of the cancel and correct dialogs, which never touch Minvoice. e2e: `test/einvoice.e2e-spec.ts` against `test/fake-minvoice.ts`, an HTTP stand-in for Minvoice (behaviours `ok`, `drop`, `drop-after-create`, `date-order`, `reject`; an invoice list filtered by `orderNumber`, whose rows leave `orderNumber` out unlike the real list, so the e2e suite covers the case where only the filter vouches for a row) that also runs on its own for browser checks.
