# Hóa đơn điện tử (frontend)

Paths are relative to `502-frontend/src/`.

- **Hóa đơn điện tử** (`/[branch]/sales/einvoices` on both sites, `components/einvoices/*`, sidebar item under Bán hàng right after Quản lý bán hàng, `layout.tsx` for the tab title; permissions `einvoices.view` (readers), `einvoices.write` (managers, cashier), `einvoices.issue` and `einvoices.config` (chain manager); specs `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-hddt-bo-cuc-va-hd-tu-do-design.md`, `2026-10-02-trang-bao-cao-hddt-design.md` (dropping the free invoices of the first) and `2026-10-02-bao-cao-theo-tung-hddt-design.md` (invoice dates and report numbers on the report site)). The page body is `components/einvoices/einvoices-page.tsx`, `EinvoicesPage({ site })`: the route of the main site (`app/[branch]/sales/einvoices/page.tsx`, `site="main"`) and that of the report site (`app/report/[branch]/sales/einvoices/page.tsx`, `site="report"`) both wrap it in `Suspense`, since it reads `useSearchParams`. Every bill is a `BillRef` (`lib/einvoice-bills.ts`: `{kind: "order" | "manual", id}`; `billRefOf(invoice)`, `billBody(ref)` — `{orderId}` or `{manualBillId}`, what a new draft is posted with —, `billUrl(ref)`, `detailRef(detail)`, `sameBill`, `billKey`). On top, the Minvoice card (`einvoice-config-card.tsx`): the chain manager sees `MST <Branch.taxCode>`, the username, a password box that is never filled in (`autoComplete="new-password"`), **Đăng nhập** and the dropdown of the year's symbols (loaded after the login; choosing one calls `PUT symbol`), and only the seller's **company name** is shown; a changed MST or a `loginError` asks to log in again, a branch without an MST links to the Cơ sở page (on the report site, which has no such page, it only names the main site's one: `useSite()`), everyone else sees one status line. **Left column** (`einvoice-bill-list.tsx`, takes `site`): `Tabs` **Bill** | Nháp | Lỗi | Không rõ | Đã xuất (counts of `GET /einvoices/summary`) filter the **bills** of `GET /einvoices/bills`; the report site reads `GET /report-site/bills` and `GET /report-site/bills/summary` instead, and both lists are mapped to one `ListedBill`: Bill = the paid bills of a `DateRangePicker` range of business days (default `businessDate()`; on the report site the bills holding an invoice whose **invoice date** is in the range, plus every bill thêm tay of those business days), Đã xuất = the bills with an issued invoice in that range (main site: `Einvoice.businessDate`, the bill's business day; report site: `invoiceDate`), Nháp/Lỗi/Không rõ = the bills holding such an invoice on any day (the picker is off; voided bills included, badge Đã hủy); a bill-number search works in every tab; `ListLimitNotice` at 500. There is no free invoice any more: no **+** beside the search and no "Hóa đơn không theo bill" group. Each bill row has its own icon **+** (`einvoices.write`, not on a voided bill) that creates a draft at once with what is left of the bill (0 when nothing is, and always 0 on a bill thêm tay, whose invoices are its total), so several can be added in a row; a bill thêm tay wears a **Thêm tay** badge and shows its day instead of the time it was paid; clicking a bill opens it in place (`bill-split.tsx`: `BillSplit` for a paid bill — VAT, discounts, đã chia, còn lại / vượt, warnings for a voided bill or one edited after its invoices —, `ManualBillSplit` for a bill thêm tay — its day, who added it and when, the reason if it was cancelled; no còn lại and no VAT line, since its total is what its invoices hold —, then its invoices by `id`, named **HĐ n** on the main site and by their report number (`reportNumber`) on the report site; `ManualBillSplit` also has **Hủy bill** (`einvoices.write`, `ReasonDialog` ≤ 300, `POST /report-site/manual-bills/:id/cancel`), after which the page reloads the list and the bill and clears the selection). Every invoice row (`einvoice-row.tsx`) has its **amount** box (drafts only; saved on Enter or blur through `PATCH` with the saved buyer, lines and date — every row reads `GET /einvoices/:id` first, so a Lưu nháp whose reload is still in flight is never overwritten), its status badge, the buyer, and a bin that deletes a draft (at once when it is empty — amount 0, no buyer, no line, `isEmptyDraft` — otherwise after a confirm). **Right column** (a full-screen `Sheet` on a phone, opened only by tapping an invoice), `einvoice-issue-panel.tsx`: "HĐ n · Bill … · phòng" (the report number instead of HĐ n on the report site; a paid bill's from its detail, a bill thêm tay's from the label the page builds), the status, the amount (read only), then by status: `einvoice-editor.tsx` for a draft (**Ngày hóa đơn**, a `DatePicker` with `min` = `minInvoiceDate` and `today` = the calendar day (`toDateInput()`), saved with the draft (every invoice has one); `buyer-fields.tsx` with **Tra** and, from a bill's second invoice on, **Chép từ HĐ trước**, which copies MST and name as a pair; `einvoice-lines.tsx`: Tên, ĐVT, SL, Đơn giá trước VAT, **Thành tiền trước VAT**, no rate to choose since every new line is `EINVOICE_VAT_RATE` (10%) and an older line of another rate shows "VAT x%", **Lấy món từ bill** for an invoice of a paid bill (a bill thêm tay has no items to take; its hours line is "Dịch vụ tính theo giờ" · Giờ, as Thêm hóa đơn vào báo cáo names it) and **Thêm dòng bù phần còn thiếu**; totals with Còn thiếu / Thừa; **Lưu nháp**; `invoiceDateProblem` warns, under the date and without blocking anything, of a date before `minInvoiceDate` or outside the symbol's year; `issue-controls.tsx` with **Xuất**, which sends a draft dated today at once and otherwise first asks "Đổi ngày hóa đơn về hôm nay?" — Hủy bỏ / **Giữ <ngày>** / **Đổi về hôm nay**, keeping a later date being the `confirmFutureDate` (there is no other confirm), an answer whose date `invoiceDateProblem` refuses disabled with the reason, and the date sent written into the invoice by the issue itself), `uncertain-box.tsx` for `UNCERTAIN` ("Lần gửi lúc …" from `sendingAt`, **Kiểm tra lại**, **Đã có — nhập số**, **Chưa có — gửi lại**, the last one disabled until 1 min after the send, `RESEND_WAIT_MS` in `lib/einvoice.ts`), `issued-view.tsx` for `ISSUED` (read only, without the lines the server now keeps; the chain manager edits the number in `edit-number-dialog.tsx`; **Gửi lại** (`einvoices.write`, not on a voided bill) always makes, after a confirm, a new draft of the same bill (a bill thêm tay's too) with its amount, buyer MST and name, dated today, the lines, address and email being typed again; the issued one stays, so the bill's đã chia counts both), a manual Tải lại for `SENDING`. The page owns the selection: it reads the open bill once for both columns (`billUrl(ref)`: `GET /einvoices/bill/:orderId`, or `GET /report-site/manual-bills/:id` for a bill thêm tay; `useApiData` with a null url fetches nothing, and reports loading again when the url comes back); opening a bill picks the first invoice of the tab it was opened from, else the first not issued, and that pick then stays through reloads. The address can open a bill, which Quản lý bán hàng of the report site uses: `?bill=<orderId>` or `?manualBill=<id>` (`linkedBill`), `?day=YYYY-MM-DD`, the day the list starts on instead of today's business day, and `?einvoice=<id>`, the invoice selected at once (all read once, at mount). Concurrent writes wait for each other: a bill's **+** is also disabled while that bill's detail reloads or one of its rows saves (reported from the blur itself, not an effect), so its default is the fresh remainder; while a row's amount saves, the panel's Lưu nháp and Xuất wait, and while the panel saves or issues, that invoice's amount box waits. The line table switches to one row per line at a 38rem `@container/einvoice` (`@min-[38rem]/einvoice`), so a 1366–1440px laptop shows the columns. The editor is keyed by the invoice and its saved date, buyer and lines (not `updatedAt`), so an amount typed in the left column never resets unsaved edits on the right while a save remounts it clean; every switch of bill or invoice asks "Bỏ thay đổi chưa lưu?" (`run()` in the page), as does `beforeunload`; a new draft is shown on the right only when the panel holds no unsaved edits. Any error but a 400 from issuing or **Kiểm tra lại** reloads and warns that the result is unknown, since Minvoice may have created the invoice. `lib/einvoice.ts` is the copy of `einvoice-math.ts`; `DatePicker` got `min` and `today` props; the Cơ sở page has an optional **Mã số thuế** box (send `null` to clear it); the bill sheet's cancel and correct dialogs warn "Bill có N hóa đơn điện tử" (never block). The page does no polling: it reloads after each write.
