# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Product direction

Karaoke 502 is a management system for a **chain of karaoke venues/restaurants** (branches `cs1`–`cs5`, "Cơ sở 1–5"). The target scope is:
- **Sales (Bán hàng)**: room sessions, ordering food/drinks into a room, checkout/billing per branch.
- **Inventory (Kho)**: stock import (nhập kho) and export (xuất kho), stock levels.
- **Accounting (Kế toán)**: revenue/bill/staff statistics, cash fund (quỹ thu/chi), and accounting reports generated inside the system.
- **Report site** (`baocao.<host>`; specs `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-trang-bao-cao-hddt-design.md` and `2026-10-02-bao-cao-theo-tung-hddt-design.md`): the same app on a host starting with `baocao.` or `baocao-`, with its own login. Its figures are those of the e-invoices, **one e-invoice at a time, on its own invoice date** (`Einvoice.invoiceDate`: a bill split into three invoices of three dates shows on three days; issuing moves an invoice to the date it is issued with), each with its own **report number** (`reportNumber`: DDMM of its invoice date when made + room + a sequence of `ReportCounter`, apart from the bill numbers): Quản lý bán hàng (one row per e-invoice: those a cashier sent with **Thêm hóa đơn vào báo cáo** on a paid bill of the main site, those split from a bill, and those of **bills thêm tay** — `ManualBill`, made there only to issue e-invoices and numbered with their first e-invoice's report number, so the main site's bill numbers never skip), Hóa đơn điện tử, and the Doanh thu / Phòng / Hàng hóa reports. Details in `502-backend/CLAUDE.md` (Report site) and `502-frontend/CLAUDE.md`.

**Accounts and permissions** — every person is a `User` (there is no separate employee table); the role grants rights on top of that. Every account except the chain manager belongs to exactly one branch and can only act within it:
- `CHAIN_MANAGER` — Quản lý hệ thống: all branches; catalog, inventory, reports, funds, accounts (including branch managers) and branches.
- `BRANCH_MANAGER` — Quản lý cơ sở: the same, but only their own branch, and only manages `CASHIER`/`STAFF` accounts. Like the cashier, they can no longer correct or void a paid bill (see `CHAIN_ONLY` below); they still cancel open sessions and approve discounts.
- `CASHIER` — Thu ngân: own branch, cashier work (open rooms, order, checkout), plus two read-only pages of their branch: the bills (Quản lý bán hàng: `GET /orders`, `GET /orders/summary`) and the revenue report (Doanh thu: `GET /reports/revenue`), all three on `SALES_READERS`. The e-invoice page of their branch (Hóa đơn điện tử) is also theirs for drafts (`EINVOICE_WRITERS` = `SALES`; issuing stays `CHAIN_ONLY`, see E-invoices). No inventory, other reports, funds, catalog or accounts. A discount raised or a VAT lowered on a bill is not applied at once: it goes to the managers as a request to approve (see Orders / billing).
- `STAFF` — Nhân viên: sees the rooms whose open session they serve (as CSKH or phục vụ). The one assigned as `serverId` (phục vụ) of a session also orders and removes items for that room, brings PR/KTV in and out of it and locks its time (`GET /products` and `GET /pr/available` are open to `STAFF` for the menu and the PR picker; `GET /products` leaves `costPrice` out for them; `isServerOf` in `src/orders/order-access.ts`, checked under the order's row lock; another room answers 403 "Bạn chỉ thao tác được phòng mình phục vụ"); a floor staff member needs a password to log in, so a phục vụ only works once their account has one. The CSKH of a room only looks.
- `CHAIN_ONLY` (`src/auth/roles.ts`) = `CHAIN_MANAGER` alone: `PATCH /orders/:id/paid` and `POST /orders/:id/void` (correcting or voiding a paid bill), and the e-invoice actions that reach Minvoice or settle its answer: issuing, resolving an uncertain one, editing an issued number, the Minvoice login and the symbol (see E-invoices).
- `BOARD` — Hội đồng quản trị (HĐQT): no branch, **view only** on every branch (reports, bills, stock, fund, rooms, accounts list; `READERS`/`SALES_READERS`/`ALL_BRANCH_ROLES` in `src/auth/roles.ts`; write routes stay on `MANAGERS`/`SALES`). Its one write is `POST /admin/purge {scope: 'branch'|'all', branch?, password}` (`src/data-purge`, BOARD only): after re-checking the caller's own password (403 on a wrong one, throttled like logins) it deletes in one interactive transaction the bills, stock documents/movements, fund entries, products, categories and rooms of that branch or of the whole system, and writes a `DataPurgeLog` row (who, scope, branch snapshot, counts, user agent) in the same transaction; a wrong password also writes a row with `success=false`. Branches, accounts and the log stay. `GET /admin/purge/logs` (chain manager and BOARD) lists the last 500. The purge also deletes `DiscountRequest` and `OrderEvent` of its scope (logged as `discountRequests`, `orderEvents`), before the orders they point at, the `Einvoice` rows of its scope (logged as `einvoices`, also before the orders; `EinvoiceConfig` stays like branches and accounts, and nothing is deleted on Minvoice), and its bills thêm tay (logged as `manualBills`, after the e-invoices). Frontend: `Xóa dữ liệu` in the `NavUser` menu (`components/purge-data-dialog.tsx`), `/[branch]/admin/purge-logs` (permission `purge.logs`); `lib/permissions.ts` gives BOARD only the view permissions (`*.view`, `catalog.view`, `users.view`, `branches.view`), so every page opens read only: write buttons check the write permission (`sales.settings`, `inventory`, `funds`, `users`, `branches`) and `ExcelImportButton` renders nothing without `imports`. Created by the chain manager (migration `20260929000000_board_role`).

`User.position` (`CSKH` | `SERVER`) is independent of the role: it marks who can be assigned to a room session. Floor staff may have no password (`password` is nullable) and then cannot log in. `User.managesPr` ("Quản lý PR/KTV", Có/Không in the account form) is also independent of the role: it lets any account of a branch keep that branch's PR/KTV list and roll call (see PR/KTV below); managers always can. `User.reportAccess` ("Vào trang báo cáo", Có/Không in the account form) lets a branch manager or BOARD use the report site; the chain manager always can, cashiers and staff never (`canUseReportSite` in `src/auth/roles.ts` ↔ `lib/permissions.ts`, `REPORT_ACCESS_ROLES` exported from both). Only the chain manager sets it, only `BRANCH_MANAGER`/`BOARD` accounts can hold it, and an account moved to any other role loses it in the same write, from the accounts page or the Excel account import.

UI text and user-facing error messages are in Vietnamese; keep new strings in Vietnamese.

## Resource rules (RAM, SSD)

The system runs on one small server (database, backend, frontend and backups together) and must hold 10 cashiers (2 per branch) working while up to 10 people download reports; selling always comes before reports. **Every change must follow `docs/resource-rules.md`**; read it before touching queries, API payloads, polling, dependencies, Docker, logs or stored files, and tick its checklist (§5) before calling the work done. In short: nothing grows forever — every list query has a `take` and `select`s only the fields the UI uses, capped lists send `X-Total-Count` (`withTotalCount`) and their screens show `ListLimitNotice`, no total is ever summed from a capped list (use a summary endpoint), totals are computed in SQL, JSON bodies stay at 1mb except the routes that mount their own parser (`/api/imports`, 5mb), report queries run on `ReportPrismaService` (own 3-connection pool) behind `SharedRequestInterceptor`, sales-flow queries use an index (no seq scan of big tables), in-memory maps have a cap, polling goes through `hooks/use-polling.ts`, heavy frontend libraries are loaded with `await import()`, build-only packages are devDependencies, and every Docker service has `logging: *logging`, a `mem_limit` (`*_MEM_LIMIT` in `.env`) and, for Node, a `--max-old-space-size` below it. Any exception gets a comment saying why.

## Repository layout

A single git repo at the root (`origin` = `https://github.com/linhsayshii/502-karaoke.git`, branch `main`) holding two independent npm projects, no workspace tooling at the root:
- `502-backend/` — NestJS 11 + Prisma 5.22 (default in-process `library` engine, not `binary`) + PostgreSQL.
- `502-frontend/` — Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui (new-york style, lucide icons).
- Docs (Vietnamese): root `README.md` (overview + quick start), root `DEPLOYMENT.md` (the only deployment guide: Docker install, Nginx/HTTPS, updates, backup/restore, migrating the legacy PM2 server incl. the one-time `foundation` upgrade, troubleshooting), `502-backend/README.md` and `502-frontend/README.md` (local development only). Keep deployment steps in `DEPLOYMENT.md`, not in the per-project READMEs.
- Root `src/app/[branch]/` is an empty leftover directory; ignore it.

## Commands

Backend commands and their gotchas are in `502-backend/CLAUDE.md`, frontend ones in `502-frontend/CLAUDE.md`; the standard scripts are in each `package.json`.

Docker (root; the details are in `docker-compose.yml`, `backup/` and `DEPLOYMENT.md`): services `db` (postgres:17), `backend`, `frontend` and `backup`, config in root `.env` (from `.env.docker.example`). Settings that look arbitrary have a reason, don't revert them: `max_parallel_workers_per_gather=0` (parallel query only fought the cashiers for CPU under load), `shm_size: 256mb` (the 64 MB default made concurrent reports fail with `could not resize shared memory segment`), the TCP healthcheck (on first init the socket answers before the DB exists; `start_interval` needs Docker Engine ≥ 25), `entrypoint.sh` of `backup` copying env vars to `/run/backup.env` (crond jobs do not inherit them). Keep the `backup` image's `FROM` at the same PostgreSQL major as `db`. Production has **no Nginx**: it is reached through a **Cloudflare Tunnel** (`cloudflared` on the server → `localhost:3000`, `APP_PORT=127.0.0.1:3000`, HTTPS and any per-IP login rate limit live in Cloudflare; requests over 100 s get a 524; the Next rewrite proxy in front of the backend cuts a request at `experimental.proxyTimeout` = 95 s in `next.config.ts`, just below that, since Next's default of 30 s turned every slower call into a 500) — `DEPLOYMENT.md` §3.2; the Nginx sections are for other installs. Only the frontend port is published; the browser calls `/api` on the same origin and the Next rewrite proxies to `http://backend:4000` (`API_PROXY_TARGET`, baked at build time; unset it defaults to production). The backend container runs `node_modules/.bin/prisma migrate deploy` on start (not `npx`; so `prisma` is a prod dependency); seed with `docker compose exec backend node dist/prisma/seed.js`.

## Environment

- Backend `.env` (see `.env.example`): `DATABASE_URL`, `PORT`, `JWT_SECRET`, `JWT_REFRESH_SECRET` (required when `NODE_ENV=production`, dev falls back to fixed secrets), `COOKIE_SECURE` (refresh cookie `secure` flag), `CORS_ORIGINS` (comma-separated), `SWAGGER_ENABLED`, `TZ` (business day is computed in server local time; use `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`), `EINVOICE_SECRET` (32 bytes, base64, `openssl rand -base64 32`: the key that encrypts the Minvoice passwords and sessions; required when `NODE_ENV=production`, where the backend refuses to start without a valid one and `docker-compose.yml` refuses to start at all without it (`:?`); dev and test fall back to a fixed key), `MINVOICE_URL_TEMPLATE` (tests and the fake Minvoice only, e.g. `http://127.0.0.1:4555/{taxCode}`; ignored in production).
- Frontend: `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` (inlined at build time). No `.env*` file is committed; without one `lib/api.ts` falls back to `http://localhost:4000/api`. Local, uncommitted `.env*` files may exist and win over that fallback — `next build` reads `.env.production`, `next dev` reads `.env.development(.local)` — so to build against a local backend set it in the shell: `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://localhost:4000/api npm run build`. `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=/api` goes through the `next.config.ts` rewrite to `API_PROXY_TARGET`, which defaults to the **production** backend (`https://kara.hvlsv.uk`) when unset.

## Architecture notes (loaded with the code they describe)

- `502-backend/CLAUDE.md` — backend architecture: auth, branch scoping, orders/billing, discounts, live events, bill numbers, business day, inventory, funds, reports, Excel import, PR/KTV, report site. `502-backend/src/einvoice/CLAUDE.md` — e-invoices (Minvoice).
- `502-frontend/CLAUDE.md` — frontend architecture: shell, report site, permissions, API client, shared pieces, reports, PR/KTV, room page, discounts. `502-frontend/src/components/einvoices/CLAUDE.md` — the Hóa đơn điện tử page.
- Read the relevant one before changing or answering questions about that area ("see Orders / billing", "see E-invoices", "see PR/KTV" above point there).

Rules that hold everywhere:
- **Never run a second backend against the production database**, not even for a test or a migration check: its start-up sweep would turn the other's live e-invoice sends into `UNCERTAIN`.
- Don't use `prisma db push`; schema changes go through migrations.
- Copies that must stay in sync, backend ↔ frontend: `src/orders/billing.ts` (`computeBill`, the single billing formula) ↔ `lib/billing.ts`; `src/einvoice/einvoice-math.ts` ↔ `lib/einvoice.ts`; `src/discounts/discount-rules.ts` ↔ `lib/discount-rules.ts`; the permission matrix (`src/auth/roles.ts`, `canUseReportSite` and `REPORT_ACCESS_ROLES` included) ↔ `lib/permissions.ts`; an import field in `lib/excel-import/definitions.ts` ↔ its backend DTO.
